How to play

Clear the board. Two hidden words guard it: one across the top, and one down the left side.

  • Tap a word to see its clue, then type your answer and press Check.
  • The brighter the word, the more it's worth.
  • Compound words glow white, pay double and burn red when you get them. Each half has its own clue, and they're the hardest words on the board.
  • Every letter you get right drops straight down its line, and it sticks wherever it fits a word below.
  • Each full bar stamps a letter into both the top word and the side word. Tap either one to guess it any time.
  • Crack the top word and its letters crash down the board. Crack the side word and its letters shoot across the rows. Either one sets the screen on fire for 10 seconds.
  • Once both are cracked, every full bar fills in a whole column: the one with the fewest letters in it.
  • You have 5 lives. A wrong guess costs one. Check letter also costs one, and locks in the right letter at your cursor.
