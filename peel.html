How to play

One hidden word, three clues. Peel a letter off one end and you get a shorter word. Peel again for a shorter one still. The words stack up like a pyramid:

AIRHAIRCHAIR
  • Tap a row to see its clue. The shortest word is on top and the full word is at the bottom.
  • Check one row at a time. Get it right and it drops down and merges into the row below, filling in those letters.
  • You have 4 misses. Revealing a letter costs one.
  • The shorter the word, the easier its clue, so the short word is a good place to start.
  • Solve the full word to win.

Puzzles get harder from Monday to Saturday. Some peel from the end instead, so the pyramid leans the other way:
